# Dentora Clinic demo - senior web designer review (visual / UX)

Method: Playwright, all 10 routes at 390x844 and 1440x900 (fold + full page), home also at 768x1024 and 1920x1080, plus drawer, popup, quiz result, before/after slider and hero-at-50%-scroll. Scroll-reveal classes removed. Screenshots in `shots/` (all names below are relative to it). Fonts are fallback in the sandbox, so face is not judged. No horizontal scroll at any tested width (scrollWidth == viewport everywhere). Console only shows blocked Google Fonts requests.

## Scores (1-10)
| Area | Score | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| Flow | 8 | Home story is right (hero > 3 treatments > proof > quiz > package > team > reviews > services > digital > contact). Mobile hero CTA below the fold and no persistent mobile CTA. |
| Width/Grid | 7.5 | 1240 container, clean left edge, good card rows. Defects at 768 (cards stuck at 560px), empty areas next to slider on treatment pages. |
| Typography | 8 | Clear H1>H2>H3 steps, good measure. Some 10-11px labels and 13px button text. |
| Mobile | 7 | No horizontal scroll, drawer and popup good, faces not cut. Hero CTA under the fold, long stacks, 37px tabs, small button text. |
| Consistency | 7 | Home and 6 service pages are one family. The 3 core treatment pages are visibly thinner than the service pages. |
| Conversion | 6.5 | Real doctor, 5.0 Google rating, reviews, before/after are strong. Visible placeholder text, no map, no sticky mobile CTA, email not a link. |
| Technical | 7.5 | Esc closes drawer and popup, tap handle 54px, no console errors beyond fonts. Blurry service-card imagery, mailto missing. |

## Findings

### Critical
1. **#aligneri and #ljuskice · all viewports · "Prije i poslije" block** - Dashed box with visible internal copy "Ovdje dolaze fotografije terapije alignerima ... Čekamo materijale i suglasnosti pacijenata od klinike" (same for ljuskice). Why: a patient-facing page shows an internal to-do; for a clinic, an empty proof section on two of the three core treatments reads as unfinished and destroys trust. Fix: until real photos exist, remove the whole section (and its anchor) or reuse the working `.cmp` slider with the izbjeljivanje pair clearly labelled as a sample; never ship the "Čekamo materijale" copy. Files: `aligneri-d-full.png`, `aligneri-m-full.png`, `ljuskice-d-full.png`.

### High
2. **Home · 390 · hero (`.hero-grid`)** - Primary CTA "Naručite se" sits at about y=870, i.e. below the 844px fold; the first screen is photo, name badge, eyebrow, H1 and half of the subtitle. Why: the guide's rule (value line + one CTA above the fold on mobile) fails on the most important screen for mobile-heavy clinic traffic. Fix: cap the photo at ~48-52svh (e.g. `.hero-photo{height:min(52svh,420px)}`) or pull the CTA up by shortening the subtitle to one line. File: `home-m-fold.png`.
3. **Home + all pages · 390 · no persistent booking/phone CTA** - After the hero, the only booking buttons are in-section; the 11,000px mobile home scrolls past ~4 screens (treatment cards, slider, quiz) with none visible and the header has only the burger. Why: CTA reach rule is ~1.5 screens. Fix: add a slim sticky bottom bar on <=768px: [Nazovi] (tel:) + [Naruči se] (`data-open-form`), 52px high, hide while the popup/drawer is open, add `padding-bottom` to footer. Files: `home-m-full.png`, `hero-scroll50-m.png`.
4. **Core treatment pages (#aligneri, #ljuskice, #izbjeljivanje) vs service pages · all** - Service pages end with the dark "Prvi korak je pregled." CTA band, "Povezani tretman" card and an "Ostale usluge" carousel; the three core pages (the money pages) end with FAQ + form + footer only, no doctor, no reviews, no related treatment. Why: weakest page template is used for the highest-value treatments; dead end without a next step except the form. Fix: reuse the service-page template pieces: add the dark CTA band, a "Povezani tretmani" card (aligneri > izbjeljivanje > ljuskice package link) and 2-3 review cards with the Google 5.0 badge. Files: `aligneri-d-full.png`, `izbjeljivanje-d-full.png`, `restaurativna-d-full.png`.
5. **Home · 768 · `.tc` treatment cards** - At 768 the three cards stack in one column, each only 560px wide (measured `.tc` width 560, container 728) and left-aligned, leaving ~190px empty on the right; images are 330px tall so the section is ~1800px. Why: looks like a bug and wastes the tablet viewport. Fix: at 600-1023px use `grid-template-columns:repeat(3,1fr)` with a shorter image (aspect 4/5) or make `.tc` `width:100%`; keep one column only <600px. File: `home-768-full.png`.
6. **Home · 1440 · "Naši radovi" (`.cmp` + process grid)** - The right column "Pregled / Plan / Tretman / Kontrola" box is vertically centred next to the slider with ~110px of dead space above it and nothing tying it to the slider (it describes the process, not the photo). Why: reads as floating filler; weakens the best proof section. Fix: top-align the column and add one line above it ("Što se dogodilo na ovom slučaju" with 2 facts: trajanje, broj posjeta), or move the 4 steps below the slider as a 4-column strip. File: `hd-01.jpg` (from `home-d-full.png`).
7. **Home · 390/1440 · "Ostale usluge" row thumbnails and service-page "Ostale usluge" carousel cards** - Parodontologija/Protetika cards are heavily blurred (out-of-focus source crops upscaled to ~330x420); the circular 48px thumbs show tiny unrecognisable crops. Why: blur reads as low quality in a section meant to show technical care. Fix: use sharp source images (or object-position on the sharp area), minimum 2x the display size, or switch cards to a flat tinted background with an icon. Files: `restaurativna-d-full.png` (bottom), `home-m-full.png`.

### Medium
8. **Home · 1440 · hero trust row `.hero-trust` (Prirodni rezultati / Individualan pristup / ...)** - Starts at x=760 on top of the doctor's white uniform edge; "DENT" logo embroidery on her sleeve collides with the first icon, labels are 12px grey on a busy cream/photo gradient. Why: low contrast and clutter right under the CTA. Fix: shift the text column 24-32px right (`padding-left`), or add a white 70% backdrop to the row; raise label to 13px and colour to the dark brown token. File: `home-d-fold.png`.
9. **Mobile · all · small text and tap size** - Button text is 13px everywhere (`.btn`), eyebrows 11px, "Pitanje 1 od 3" 10px, treatment tabs `[role=tab]` are 37px tall (88-115px wide), "Prikaži više" is a bare text link. Why: below 44px tap and below the 14-16px comfortable size for patients 40+. Fix: `.btn{font-size:14px}` on mobile, tabs `min-height:44px`, quiz counter 11px min, "Prikaži više" `padding:12px 0`. Files: `home-m-fold.png`, `slider-m.png`.
10. **Home · 1440 · "Naš tim" and contact** - Contact section is thin: address/phone/email are plain text (the email is not a `mailto:` link, phone is only a button), no map, no parking/transport note, no social. Footer is one line (copyright + Politika privatnosti) with no logo, nav or phone. Why: users who scroll to the bottom need to find and navigate to the clinic; Google Maps embed/link is a major trust and conversion signal. Fix: add a Google Maps embed or static map tile in the right column under the info card, `mailto:` and `tel:` on the values, "Upute za dolazak" link, and a 3-column footer (logo + address, nav, hours + phone). Files: `hd-04.jpg`, `home-m-full.png`.
11. **Home · all · slider `.cmp` labels** - "POSLIJE" label sits at the top right and the divider passes through it when the handle is dragged to ~80% (the label is crossed by the white line); at 390 the after photo area gets very narrow. Why: label collision looks buggy. Fix: clamp the divider to 12-88% or hide a label when the divider overlaps it. File: `slider-m.png`.
12. **Home · 1440 · dark "Digitalna ordinacija" section** - Image is vertically centred against a taller text column and the two headings are on different baselines (image top ~170px below the H2 top); H2 breaks "Bez gumenih / otisaka. Bez / nagađanja." into three ragged lines. Intraoral/3D/RTG labels wrap into two lines in a narrow 100px column. Fix: `align-items:start` and sticky or top-aligned image, `text-wrap:balance` on the H2 so it breaks "Bez gumenih otisaka. / Bez nagađanja.", widen the label column to 160px. File: `dig.jpg` (`home-d-full.png` y 6300).
13. **Home · 1440 · reviews row** - Cards have unequal heights (first card is short and untruncated, others end with "Prikaži više"), the scroller clips the next card at the container edge instead of bleeding to the viewport edge, and nothing shows it is scrollable besides the cut card (no arrows/dots). Fix: `align-items:stretch`, same line-clamp (e.g. 6 lines) on all cards, let the track bleed to the window edge, add prev/next arrows like the service carousel. File: `hd-03.jpg`.
14. **Home · 390 · core treatments and team** - Three full-width treatment cards with 200px photos stack to ~1100px; the "Sva tri tretmana" package steps stack another 3 cards; services list rows are 3-line titles ("Restaurativna stomatologija i endodoncija" in 3 lines). Why: the page is ~11,000px; sections are too long on phone. Fix: make the three treatment cards a horizontal snap scroller with 85% card width (peek), shorten the long service names on mobile (keep full on the page H1). File: `home-m-full.png`.
15. **#aligneri / #ljuskice / #izbjeljivanje · 1440 · before/after block** - The slider/placeholder is ~2/3 width and left aligned, right third is empty, thumbnails beneath are small. On izbjeljivanje the block feels unbalanced next to the full-width 2-column sections above and below. Fix: put a text/benefit column to the right (results timing, "Želim ovakav osmijeh" CTA) or centre and enlarge to the container width. File: `izbjeljivanje-d-1.jpg`.
16. **Home · 1440 · hero curtain at 50% scroll** - The next section covers the hero text while the H1 is half cut ("osmijehom" cropped by the section edge) and both CTAs disappear within ~250px of scrolling, so for most of the scroll depth the page has only the header button. Fix: acceptable effect, but have the header "Naručite se" stay visible (it does) and start the next section's overlap slightly later, or fade hero text out before it is clipped. File: `hero-scroll50-d.png`.

### Low / polish
17. Home · 390 · doctor badge (`.hero-badge`) sits tight under the header and overlaps the photo top; give it 12px top gap or move it to the lower-left like desktop. `home-m-fold.png`.
18. Section transitions on all pages show a soft dark shadow band at each section top (e.g. between cream sections); it looks like a smudge at 1440 and 1920. Use a 1px border or subtle gradient only. `hd-01.jpg`, `hd-03.jpg`.
19. Home · 1920 - content stays at 1240 with full-bleed hero; looks calm and not stretched, but the hero doctor photo is cropped to the torso and the badge is far left of the text; consider `max-width:1600px` on the hero. `home-1920-fold.png`.
20. Service pages · 390 - card grids (Ekstrakcija / Alveotomija / Apikotomija) have three identical stacked white cards with a tiny gold dot; add icons or a 2-col layout so the page is shorter. `ok.jpg`.
21. Drawer · 390 - good, but the "Naručite se" button in the drawer is lower contrast than the header one (looks disabled); use the solid gold with white text. `drawer-m.png`.
22. Popup · all - fields are comfortable, but labels are 10-11px caps in light grey (low contrast); raise to 12px and darker. Esc closes it (verified). `popup-m.png`, `popup-d.png`.
23. Quiz result · 390/1440 - result card works well; add a booking button and a link to the matching treatment page directly under the result text (check the screenshot: the CTA falls under the first screen of the card). `quiz-result-m.png`, `quiz-result-d.png`.

## Keep
1. **Hero** - real doctor portrait, one-line promise with italic accent word, primary + secondary CTA and the 4-icon trust row on desktop; face is never cut at 390/768/1440/1920.
2. **Home story order** - treatments > before/after > quiz > package > team > reviews > services > digital > contact, with alternating cream / white / sand / dark sections.
3. **Before/after slider with 3 cases and tabs** - 54px handle, drag hint, thumbnail switcher.
4. **"Koji tretman je za mene?" quiz** - 3 questions, progress bar, personalised result.
5. **Service-page template** - breadcrumb, arch image with doctor badge, anchor chips, numbered process timeline, dark "Prvi korak je pregled." band, related-service carousel.
6. **Booking popup and mobile drawer** - short form (name, phone, treatment, optional message), closes with Esc, drawer has clear CTA + phone, tap targets 46px+.
7. **Tight, consistent button/radius system** (gold fill primary, outline secondary, 52px high) and gold eyebrows with a leading rule.
